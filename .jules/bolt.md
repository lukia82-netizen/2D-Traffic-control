## 2025-03-05 - [VehicleRenderer rendering loop optimization]
**Learning:** High-frequency rendering loops (running at 60 FPS in `VehicleRenderer.ts`) are highly sensitive to garbage collection overhead and redundant operations. Specifically, MapLibre's `map.getBounds()` and its getter methods like `bounds.getWest()`, `getEast()`, etc., internally perform calculations and allocate objects. Hoisting these boundaries and variables like `pxPerMetre` outside per-entity rendering loops significantly decreases frame rendering times. Additionally, avoiding the allocation of temporary coordinate arrays (e.g. `[lng, lat]`) and reusable class-level properties (such as the `renderedIds` Set) reduces GC pauses, leading to smoother animations. Pre-populating a mapping (`laneGroupCache`) of lane IDs to road groups during initial map loading avoids expensive $O(E)$ spatial searches, converting them to $O(1)$ lookups on every frame.
**Action:** When working with high-frequency rendering frameworks or animation loops, identify expensive library calls, coordinate/object allocations, or spatial checks that can be hoisted, cached, or refactored into native lookup tables/objects. Always prefer passing objects directly if their shape matches the expected type (e.g. `{ lat, lng }` compatibility with MapLibre's `LngLatLike` projection).
