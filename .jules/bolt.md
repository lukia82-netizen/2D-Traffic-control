## 2026-05-10 - O(1) Road Group Filtering
**Learning:** O(E) spatial searches (`resolveVehicleGroup`) for road-group filtering of hidden roads represent a significant CPU bottleneck when rendering thousands of vehicles. By pre-computing a static `laneGroupCache` mapping lane IDs to road groups during `setEdgeIndex` (using node OSM IDs as the common join key between edges and lanes), we can resolve road groups in O(1) per frame for any vehicle with a `currentLaneId`. This entirely bypasses the expensive spatial calculations and coordinates distance checking.
**Action:** Always prefer indexing static map relationships (such as lane to road type, lane to speed limit, etc.) during initialization/loading phases instead of performing high-frequency geometric/spatial queries during the per-frame render loop.
