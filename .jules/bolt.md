## 2026-06-25 - O(1) Road-Group Filtering and Allocation-Free Projection
**Learning:** In 'VehicleRenderer.ts', determining the visibility of a vehicle (based on road group toggles) was previously an (E)$ spatial search or a throttled cache. By using the 'currentLaneId' available in 'VehicleState' and pre-computing a lane-to-road-group map, we can achieve (1)$ lookups per vehicle per frame. Additionally, casting entity state objects to 'any' for 'this.map.project()' avoids thousands of temporary '[lng, lat]' array allocations per frame.
**Action:** Always check for entity-level identifiers (like 'laneId') that can be used for O(1) attribute lookups before falling back to spatial searches in rendering loops. Minimize object/array allocations in 60FPS loops by reusing or casting existing objects.
