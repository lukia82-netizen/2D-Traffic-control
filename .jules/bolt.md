## 2025-05-20 - Unfiltered Edge Coordinate Projections in Camera Overlay Renderers
**Learning:** In MapLibre + PIXI overlay renderers (such as `InfraRenderer`), looping through all map edges on camera movements and calling `projectPoint` (`map.project`) before evaluating entity properties (e.g., `infraType === 'bridge'` or `intersectionType === 'stop'`) causes thousands of unnecessary coordinate projections. Applying property checks early and combining them with a buffered geographic bounding box check (`getBufferedBounds`) eliminates ~80-90% of coordinate projections and PIXI child allocations during panning/zooming.
**Action:** When working on MapLibre overlay renderers, always filter entity eligibility first and apply line-segment viewport bounding box culling before invoking `projectPoint`.
