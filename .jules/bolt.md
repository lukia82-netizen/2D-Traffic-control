## 2025-05-24 - Line Segment Viewport Culling & Screen Point Reuse in RoadRenderer
**Learning:** When implementing viewport bounding box culling for line segments (roads), testing `fromOutside && toOutside` causes edges that span across the viewport to be incorrectly culled. The correct check is verifying whether both endpoints lie on the *same* side outside the padded bounding box (`bothWest || bothEast || bothSouth || bothNorth`). Furthermore, reusing already projected screen coordinates (`r.from`, `r.to`) in subsequent rendering passes (such as junction circle drawing) eliminates thousands of redundant `projectPoint` calls.
**Action:** Always check axis-aligned endpoint bounds on the same side for line culling, and reuse projected screen points across rendering passes in MapLibre/Pixi overlays.
